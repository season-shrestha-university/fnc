Principles

Transform and opacity

Nothing else moves, except the check-in tick (stroke-dashoffset) and everyday colour on toggles and buttons.

Exits are faster

Leave at 70% of the time it took to arrive, so the door can get back to scanning.

No decoration

No bounce, no spinning loaders, no gradient shimmer. Skeletons pulse opacity only.

Reduced motion

Distances and scales go to zero. What remains is a 120ms fade, or an instant change.
